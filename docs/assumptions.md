# Assumptions and Constraints


## Assumptions
```
- Retailer product pages are publicly accessible.
- Product information can be collected without authentication.
- Retailers expose enough information to identify products consistently.
- Daily collection is sufficient for the pricing team's needs.
```

## Constraints
```
- Only espresso machines are in scope.
- The initial system supports five specified retailers.
- The crawler runs once per day.
- Expected volume is approximately 500 product observations per day.
```
